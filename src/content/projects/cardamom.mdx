Cardamom is a beloved local restaurant in Ann Arbor, located about 10 minutes from Central Campus. Despite their exceptional food and community atmosphere, their website's **confusing navigation and inefficient ordering process** was creating barriers. Additionally, the cafe was **struggling to attract students** due to the distance from campus.

This 6-week individual project focused on redesigning the complete e-commerce experience to **replace their phone-based ordering system** with a modern, digital solution that maintains their warm, authentic brand identity.

- Allow users to browse menu, add items to cart, and checkout digitally
- Maintain Cardamom's **warm, authentic brand identity**

### What I learned

<TwoColumn leftTitle="Strength" leftIcon="check" rightTitle="Issues" rightIcon="alert">
<div slot="left">

- Users found the prototype **easy to navigate and intuitive** overall

</div>
<div slot="right">

- Participants wanted an **easier way to navigate back and forth** during checkout

</div>

I organized feedback from the usability tests in an **affinity diagram**: I reviewed each participant’s session, grouped similar notes on what worked well and what caused confusion, and translated the patterns into **actionable insights** for the next prototype iteration.

<Figure
  src={affinity}

### What made this project unique?

This redesign focused on transforming a real local restaurant’s outdated website into a **fully functional online ordering experience that didn’t previously exist**. It combined UX research, usability testing, and visual design to solve a genuine business pain point while maintaining Cardamom’s authentic brand identity and cultural essence.
