**DPSS: A Great Place to Work** is a UX design project completed through Atlas Digital Consulting Group, where our team partnered with the University of Michigan’s Division of Public Safety and Security to design a **careers-focused microsite**. The goal was to clearly communicate DPSS’s values, highlight career opportunities beyond law enforcement, and create a more approachable, community-centered recruitment experience.

This was a **design-only project**, with a team composed entirely of UX designers and no developers.

### Competitive analysis

Our team reviewed **six career sites** (Hilton, Marriott, Coca-Cola, Synchrony, Michigan State Police and Ohio State Patrol). The analysis revealed a clear gap: public safety career sites tend to **emphasize functionality over emotional connection**, while hospitality sites excel at human storytelling but often **lack institutional credibility**.

### User flow

We mapped the primary candidate journey so our designs would guide visitors **from initial interest to application submission with minimal friction**.

<ProcessSteps
  label="Primary user flow"
  steps={[
    {
      title: 'Why Join DPSS',

    },
    {
      title: 'Explore Opportunities',

    },
  ]}

**Key flow decisions**

- **Progressive Engagement:** 'Why Join DPSS' → 'Explore Opportunities' → 'Apply'
- **Reduced Cognitive Load:** Visual step-by-step application graphic
- **Flexible Pathways:** Multiple application options (portal + HR contact)
- **Ongoing Connection:** Newsletter signup to nurture longer-term candidates

**How the mood board helped us**

- Visualize 'approachable professionalism' before designing a single screen
- Align stakeholders on emotional tone early in the process
- Establish visual guardrails for our design system
- Bridge the gap between UMich's brand guidelines and DPSS's unique community-focused identity

This foundation ensured our final designs didn't just function well; they felt right for the audience and organization.
